# GarminDesk 0.7.0

**Release candidate · build 16 · macOS 14+**

## Understand readings at a glance

- Sleep duration now includes a quality scale and Garmin's assessment from the same recorded night. Missing or mismatched sleep scores remain explicitly unavailable.
- Summary and dedicated widgets show scale markers and short assessments for primary and supporting measurements. High stress and high energy have different meanings on their scales.
- Training load displays a verified personal range when supplied, or Garmin's acute/chronic load-ratio category. The app does not infer a universal target from the raw load number.
- Steps show progress against the recorded daily goal, with 100% appearing only once the goal is reached. Sleep-stage scales show their share of the same night's sleep without assigning a health grade.
- Historical values retain their dates. Small and large widgets reserve room for dates and connection notices instead of clipping content.

## Consistent dashboard

- Cards in each dashboard row share a height, with aligned headers, values and scale positions.
- Large widgets keep four supporting values; medium widgets keep two, with space for readable assessments and record dates.
- Detailed explanations and accessibility context remain available, preserving the existing twelve-language support.
- Invalid readiness scores no longer displace a valid primary measurement; a zero recovery countdown remains valid.
- Reopening a retained app window resumes visible metric updates, including an immediate close and reopen. Hidden and minimized windows still pause their display timers.

This candidate includes the latest 0.6.0 synchronization, account-isolation, historical-record recovery, display-timer and compatibility fixes. The repository also includes the static website source.

## Validation and distribution

Local native tests, synthetic widget/dashboard rendering and package verification are recorded in the [candidate validation report](validation/0.7.0-candidate.md). GitHub Actions builds Apple Silicon and Intel packages and checks older supported runtimes before preparing the draft.

Packages are ad-hoc signed without Apple notarization. The release remains a draft until its validation and publication steps are completed. Synthetic rendering does not establish live Garmin sign-in or the behavior of every existing desktop widget after an upgrade.
