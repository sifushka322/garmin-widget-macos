## Garmin connection and recovery

- After midnight, GarminDesk can retrieve yesterday's completed sleep and HRV when an older reading is already cached. The app and widgets display the date of the recovered night.
- When a saved session expires, GarminDesk opens the official Garmin sign-in window. With a saved login, it fills the form and waits for Garmin to enable the sign-in button. The window remains available for manual sign-in or verification.
- Diagnostics identify unfamiliar response formats and blocked Garmin sign-in pages. The app observes the retry interval when Garmin limits sign-in requests.

## Validation status

On an Apple Silicon Mac, the installed app recovered the last completed night from a pre-upgrade cache. Sleep duration, sleep score, overnight HRV and the record date matched Garmin Connect in the app and in the snapshot supplied to WidgetKit.

On the same Mac, the installed app signed in to Garmin from a fresh, nonpersistent web session using its saved login. The check verified that the app submitted the form automatically and received an authenticated Garmin Connect profile.

An upgrade using the final download with existing desktop widgets and all 15 gallery previews has not been checked manually. [Publication validation report](https://github.com/sifushka322/garmin-widget-macos/blob/main/docs/releases/validation/0.7.2-publication.md)
