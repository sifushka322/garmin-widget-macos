# Widget metric selection

Local product review, September 16, 2026. The selection policy below describes the current source in `Sources/Shared/WidgetMetricPolicy.swift`. It does not mean a new package has been released or that device compatibility has been exhaustively tested.

## Related metric priorities

| Widget | Ordered metric IDs | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day | `bodyBattery`, `steps`, `stress`, `sleepDuration`, `restingHeartRate`, `sleepScore`, `intensityMinutes`, `activeCalories`, `distance` | Daily energy, movement, and supporting health readings. |
| Sport | `trainingReadiness`, `recoveryTime`, `trainingLoad`, `vo2Max`, `intensityMinutes`, `activeCalories`, `hrv`, `bodyBattery`, `steps`, `distance` | Training-related measurements only. Completed and planned workout records belong in Training. |
| Sleep | `sleepDuration`, `sleepScore`, `deepSleep`, `remSleep`, `lightSleep`, `hrv`, `restingHeartRate`, `respiration`, `awakeSleep` | The latest completed night, with duration and score before the detailed sleep stages and supporting measurements. |

These priorities apply to the three dedicated measurement types. **Summary**, the first type, is one coherent layout with an editable ordered measurement selection; large shows a main value and up to six supporting values. Its defaults are Body Battery, steps, sleep duration, stress, readiness, resting heart rate, sleep score, intensity minutes, active calories, recovery time, and HRV, in that order. Only selected available measurements appear, limited by widget size. Unchecked measurements never return as filler. The preferred main value is first; if absent, the next selected available value takes its place. There are no profiles, assignments, content modes, or availability toggles. **Training** alone contains the week/month calendar. These priorities are product judgment, not a ranking supported by usage analytics. The repository has no evidence that a missing measurement is unpopular.

Weight, hydration, and Pulse Ox do not occupy the fixed default slots. They can be selected explicitly in Summary, without changing the dedicated types. The steps goal already contributes to the steps progress display; it need not occupy a second card. Prefer active calories over showing both total and active calories in a small overview.

## Availability and meaning

- **Body Battery** depends on compatible hardware and continuing heart-rate, HRV, and movement observations. It is a useful Overview primary metric when present, not a universal device capability. [Garmin Body Battery](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/garmin-technology/health-science/body-battery/).
- **Readiness and recovery** are training-focused measurements. Garmin readiness combines sleep, recovery time, HRV status, load, and recent sleep/stress history. This app currently reads both readiness and recovery time from its readiness response, so missing readiness data can also leave recovery time absent. Do not calculate a replacement readiness score from the remaining fields. [Garmin training readiness](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals-apac/webhelp/forerunner970/EN-SG/GUID-ECEE4CF4-51AA-413B-9FC1-6862A111E599-1964.html), [recovery time](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/garmin-technology/running-science/physiological-measurements/recovery-time/).
- **Training load** is the acute load field in the current adapter, not workout count, elapsed training time, or total load calculated by adding recent activities. Device generation and available training data affect availability. [Garmin training load](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/garmin-technology/cycling-science/physiological-measurements/training-load/).
- **Intensity minutes** are Garmin's intensity-weighted activity measure. The adapter calculates moderate minutes plus twice vigorous minutes from the daily summary. They are not the elapsed duration of workout records. Requirements vary by device. [Garmin intensity minutes](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/aviation/faq/pNU9nnDzzGAHmEavp9rpY8/).
- **HRV** here is `lastNightAvg`, an overnight average in milliseconds. It is not Garmin's HRV status classification, which requires a personal baseline. Missing status and missing nightly average are different conditions. [Garmin HRV status](https://support.garmin.com/en-CA/navionics/faq/HnFAR4oFRF4kHeqYme3bU6/).
- **VO₂ max** here is Garmin's generic/running estimate. The adapter does not expose a cycling-specific estimate. Qualifying activity requirements vary by device; the value need not change every day. [Garmin VO₂ max requirements](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=lWqSVlq3w76z5WoihLy5f8&productID=125677&tab=).
- **Hydration and weight** require separate recorded input. Hydration tracks logged servings; weight can come from a scale, a manual weigh-in, or a linked service. An absent day-scoped value does not establish that the person never tracks it. Their input requirements make them less dependable fixed widget content; those groups are requested only when explicitly selected in Summary. [Garmin hydration tracking](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-9CC4A873-E034-4A06-B2E0-636DCFE760EE/EN-US/GUID-976C04FF-A4A4-4925-AA4F-34E44C4D468A.html), [Garmin weight data](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/marine/faq/zS4yMMr7REAVHBPYY6DaZ5/).

## Display and migration rules

Automatic available-reading selection fills limited widget space using the fixed priorities above. Zero is valid. The first available candidate becomes the displayed primary with its actual label. If no candidate has a usable value, the policy retains the type's default primary without adding empty secondary cards; connection and waiting states still apply. Do not infer device support from one failed or empty refresh. Summary filters only its own ordered selection, so a missing readiness reading cannot suppress another selected sleep reading.

Preserve the dates and freshness context of retained readings. Yesterday's steps must not become today's progress. New preferences retain language, app appearance, widget appearance, Summary’s ordered selection, and refresh cadence; legacy user profiles and metric assignments are intentionally replaced by the fixed purposes. A private compatibility projection in the widget snapshot supports an old extension process during replacement without reintroducing profiles into settings. See the [simplification audit](widget-simplification-audit.md).

Training should have a distinct visual identity and a calendar presentation of actual records. A day outside retrieved calendar coverage is unknown, not an empty schedule. The latest 20 completed activities do not prove complete historical coverage. Never synthesize scheduled workouts from templates or infer missed workouts merely from the absence of a completion record.
